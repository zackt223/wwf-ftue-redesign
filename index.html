1

Choice overload on first open

The first screen asks a new player to choose a mode, answer an invite, or explore events. Every option slows down the one thing that matters: placing a first word.

Evidence: screen 1 (callouts 1–3).

Fix: one button, "Play your first word." Modes, events and invites appear after the first word is played.

2

A first match you have to figure out alone

No coach marks for dragging tiles, bonus squares or the Play button. Boosts and coins are shown with no explanation. The opponent has already moved.

Evidence: screen 3 (callouts 6–8).

Fix: the new player moves first. Three short tips appear at the moment each one is needed, and each can be dismissed.

3

Dead-end first turns

An invalid word still shows a point preview, and a rack of three Es offers no way forward. "All vowels" racks are a recurring theme in reviews about rigged tiles.

Evidence: screen 4 (callouts 10–11); 11 reviews about unfair tiles.

Fix: a balanced opening rack that can always make a 4-letter word. No score preview on invalid words. A free hint is offered after two misses.

4

Ads before value

A casino banner shows during the first turn, and players describe an ad after every move. A new player meets the monetization before they've enjoyed the game.

Evidence: screen 4 (callout 9); ads in 57.6% of negative reviews.

Fix: no ads in the first match, and no real-money gambling ads before the player's age is known. Test the revenue cost against D7.

04 · The redesign

One goal: a first word in under 60 seconds, against someone who plays back.

The prototype at the top of this page is the redesigned flow. It's built to feel like a native app: tiles track your finger 1:1, carry your throw velocity into a spring, and can be grabbed again mid-flight.

Purpose

One button on first open

"Play your first word" replaces the mode picker, invite banner and event tiles. Sign-in lives behind a text link for returning players.

Familiarity

Learn inside a real match

Three tips, shown in context: drag to the star, bonus squares, tap Play. The real board, scoring and bonus colors are kept, so nothing has to be re-learned later.

Feedback

Validate as you place

The point badge appears only when every word is valid. Errors name the problem ("Leave no gaps between letters"), and a free hint is offered after two misses.

Responsibility

Ask at the right moment

Saving progress and notifications come after turn 2, tied to a reason ("Tell me when Sam plays"). Simulated opponents are labeled.

05 · Product spec

Guided First Match

Problem

New players meet choices, unexplained systems and ads before they've played a word. US active users fell about 36% across the second half of 2025, so every new install has to count.

Goal

Raise D1 retention of new installs by 2 points or more, with a median time to first word under 60 seconds and no loss in D7 retention.

In scope
  • Single-button first open; modes and events unlock after the first word
  • New player moves first, with three contextual tips
  • Balanced opening rack and a free first hint
  • First opponent: online now, within 2 levels, labeled if simulated
  • Ad-free first match (test arm C)
  • Save-progress and notification prompt after turn 2
Out of scope
  • Returning and reactivated players
  • Ad frequency after the first match
  • Store, coins, Rewards Pass and boost economy
  • Word Search and other solo modes
Acceptance criteria
AC1

Given a first-time install, when the app opens, then one primary button ("Play your first word") is shown, with no mode picker, invite banner or event tiles.

AC2

Given the player taps it, then a board loads within 3 s (p90), the player moves first, and tip 1 points to the center star.

AC3

Given the opening rack, then it has 2–4 vowels and can make at least one common 4-letter word. No all-vowel or all-consonant rack in the first 3 turns.

AC4

Given tiles are placed, when any formed word is invalid, then no point badge shows and the message names the word. After 2 invalid attempts, the free Hint is highlighted.

AC5

Given a tip is showing, when the player taps "Hide tips", then all remaining tips are suppressed for this install and ftue_tips_hidden logs the step.

AC6

Given matchmaking for a first match, then the opponent was active in the last 5 minutes and is within 2 levels. A simulated opponent is labeled "Practice partner."

AC7

Given arm C, when fewer than 10 turns of the first match have been played, then no interstitial or banner ad renders.

AC8

Given the player's second word is played, then the save sheet appears once, can be swiped away, and guest progress is kept either way.

Risks
  • Fewer early impressions lower day-0 ad revenue
  • Too few online players at off-peak hours in small markets
  • Deferring events could reduce early Rewards Pass conversion
Mitigations
  • Revenue guardrail; judge on 30-day revenue per install, not day 0
  • Fall back to a labeled practice partner after 10 s
  • Introduce events in session 2 with a one-time spotlight

If new players get one clear button, learn inside a real first match, and don't see ads until it ends, D1 retention rises by at least 2 points, because they reach a played word sooner and leave with a game in progress.

ArmExperienceWhat it isolates
A · ControlToday's flowBaseline

Design
  • Unit: new installs, randomized by device at first open, split 1:1:1
  • Baseline: 27% D1, the GameAnalytics top-quartile benchmark. Replace with the internal baseline before launch.
  • Sample: about 9,600 installs per arm (28,800 total) to detect +2 pts at 80% power, two-sided α = 0.025 per comparison (Bonferroni for 2 comparisons). That's roughly a week of US downloads at Sensor Tower's estimates.
  • Duration: enroll for 14 days to cover two weekly cycles, then wait 7 more days for D7.
  • Checks: daily sample-ratio test; no early reads of the primary metric.
Decision rules
  • Ship C if D1 rises 2+ pts vs A (p < 0.025) and every guardrail holds.
  • Ship B if B wins but C fails the revenue guardrail. Then test a shorter ad-free window, such as 5 turns.
  • Iterate if time to first word drops but D1 doesn't move. Next, look at the wait between turns 1 and 2.
  • Stop early only if crash-free sessions or D1 in any arm falls more than 2 pts below control.
